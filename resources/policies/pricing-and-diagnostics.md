# Pricing and Diagnostics

All prices include parts and labour and are in EUR. Fixed prices apply to the exact model in our price list. "From" prices are starting prices for that kind of device; the final price depends on the model and is confirmed before we start.

Water damage, devices that do not turn on and data recovery always need a diagnosis first, because the cause is not visible from outside. The diagnostic fee is €15 and it is waived if the customer goes ahead with the repair. Diagnosis takes 1-2 working days.

Quotes above €250 are confirmed by a technician before we send them to the customer.

We never start a repair before the customer accepts the price. Discounts are not given through automatic messages.
