# Warranty

Every Blumond repair has a 90-day warranty on the repaired part, starting on the day the customer picks up the device.

The warranty covers the same problem coming back with normal use, for example a replaced battery that drains fast again or a new screen with touch problems. Warranty repairs are free of charge.

The warranty does not cover new damage after the repair: drops, cracks, bent frames or liquid damage. A technician inspects the device to confirm this.

The warranty only covers the part we repaired. Other problems are a new repair with a new quote.

To claim the warranty, bring the device and the job number from the receipt (format REP-1234).
