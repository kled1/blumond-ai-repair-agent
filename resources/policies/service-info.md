# Service Information

Blumond repairs phones, tablets, laptops, desktop computers, game consoles and smartwatches. We do not repair household appliances, TVs or cars.

Opening hours: Monday to Saturday, 09:00-19:00. Walk-ins are welcome; screen and battery repairs for common phone models are often done the same day.

Customers can send a WhatsApp message with a photo of the device to get a quote before coming to the shop.

Please back up your data before the repair when possible. Our technicians do not look at personal data, and we only ask for the device passcode when a test requires it.

For legal and security reasons we never remove iCloud, Google account or FRP locks and never change IMEI numbers without proof of ownership checked in person at the shop.

Devices that are ready must be picked up within 30 days. We send a WhatsApp message when the device is ready.
